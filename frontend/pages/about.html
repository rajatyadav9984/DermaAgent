RESEARCH & ARCHITECTURE

About DermaAgent

DermaAgent is an AI-assisted skin lesion image classification research prototype designed to explore deep learning interpretability, predictive uncertainty estimation, and multi-agent clinical decision support.

Core Machine Learning Models

  • HAM10000 7-Class Baseline (ResNet18): Trained on 10,015 dermatoscopic images across 7 diagnostic categories (Actinic Keratosis, Basal Cell Carcinoma, Benign Keratosis, Dermatofibroma, Melanoma, Melanocytic Nevi, Vascular Lesion).
  • ISIC 2019 8-Class Classifier (ResNet18 Transfer Learning): Trained on 25,331 challenge images incorporating Squamous Cell Carcinoma (SCC) with strict lesion-level patient separation.

Interpretability & Uncertainty

Standard deep learning models often produce overconfident false predictions on out-of-distribution or noisy images. DermaAgent integrates:

  • Shannon Entropy Quantification: Mathematically bounds prediction uncertainty into Low, Moderate, and High tiers.
  • Grad-CAM Visual Heatmaps: Highlights the exact visual lesion regions influencing convolutional layer activations.

Medical & Legal Disclaimer

DermaAgent is strictly an educational and technical research tool. It does not provide a medical diagnosis or treatment plan. Always consult a certified dermatologist or licensed medical healthcare provider for any skin condition assessment.
